About KIU

Kutaisi International University is a premier institution of higher learning committed to fostering academic excellence, innovation, and personal development. Our comprehensive student portal connects thousands of students to essential academic resources, community engagement, and career opportunities.

With state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and a supportive learning environment, KIU prepares students to become leaders in their respective fields. Our diverse student body represents over 30 countries, creating a truly global educational experience.
